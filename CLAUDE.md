## Design Context

### Users
Primary visitors are recruiters, hiring managers, and HR reviewers evaluating Gal Mankedi for freelance or full-time opportunities. They are scanning quickly for evidence of technical skill, delivery reliability, and overall professionalism. The interface should help them understand the candidate's stack, experience, project quality, and communication clarity fast, then guide them toward later hiring actions such as reviewing a resume PDF, reaching out, or moving forward with interviews.

### Brand Personality
The brand should feel modern, techy, and hireable. The tone should be confident, clear, and polished rather than flashy or overly playful. The interface should evoke competence, momentum, and trust, with enough personality to feel memorable without undermining professional credibility.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a light-to-deep visual progression, consistent with the current single-page scrolling portfolio structure. Favor a teal, orange, and sand palette with muted, refined application rather than loud saturation. Preserve the current direction of soft gradients, glass-like surfaces, and purposeful motion, but keep the overall result readable, recruiter-friendly, and clearly professional. No anti-reference was specified, so avoid generic template-looking portfolio patterns by default while also avoiding visual gimmicks that reduce clarity or hiring confidence.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize hireability over decoration: every visual choice should strengthen credibility, readability, and proof of capability.
2. Communicate technical competence quickly: surface stack, project scope, and delivery outcomes in a way that supports rapid scanning.
3. Keep the palette distinctive but controlled: use teal, orange, and sand to create identity without sacrificing contrast or professionalism.
4. Use motion with restraint: animation should support hierarchy and polish, and always respect reduced-motion preferences.
5. Maintain accessible clarity: preserve strong text contrast, predictable structure, and WCAG AA-oriented readability across desktop and mobile.
